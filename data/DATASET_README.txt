NASA/   Original NASA battery aging files B0005, B0006, B0007, B0018 (.mat)
        Source: NASA Ames Prognostics Center of Excellence, Prognostics Data Repository (B. Saha and K. Goebel, "Battery data set", 2007).

CALCE_processed/   One row per discharge cycle for CALCE CS2_35 to CS2_38, extracted from the original CALCE .xlsx files by the notebook:
        cap    discharge capacity (Ah)
        tv     time from 3.8 V to 3.6 V (s)
        v300   voltage at 300 s (V)
        vm600  mean voltage over 0-600 s (V)
        dv0    early voltage drop (V)
        The original CALCE .xlsx files are not included; download them from the CALCE Battery Research Group, University of Maryland (CS2 cells).

The datasets remain subject to the terms of their original providers.
